HeatSeeker Thermal AI Unit

Edge thermal camera node, rev 0.9. A Raspberry Pi 5 runs AI inference on FLIR Lepton frames while an STM32L452 supervises power, the watchdog and every peripheral rail.

  • UPS power path: 230 V AC into a RAC25-05SK 5 V supply, Li-ion backup through BQ25606 + TPS61022 boost, and TPS2121 automatic failover between them.
  • Two uplinks: SIM7600X-H LTE on a mini-PCIe socket, with a Wio-E5 LoRa module as the low-power fallback.
  • TXB0108 translation between the 3.3 V system and 1.8 V radio logic; TPS54335 and AP2112K rails that the STM32 can power-cycle.
  • Companion flex PCB, plus Python tools for the video stream, motion capture and modem readout.

Rail vehicle OEM2025HW

BK8000 Valve Drive

Five-channel closed-loop current controller for proportional valves and solenoids on rail vehicles, built to EN 50155 around an STM32G491. Revision A04 went to production.

  • Each channel: high-side shunt read by an AD8210 monitor and an LTC2312-14 14-bit ADC, driving an LTC4441 MOSFET gate driver under PID control.
  • Hardware overcurrent latch: an LT1671 comparator (60 ns) disables the gate driver without waiting for firmware, and the MCU has to reset it explicitly.
  • LTC4367 front end rides out −40 V reverse polarity and 100 V transients; an LT3433 buck-boost holds the 5 V and 3.3 V rails through cranking dips.
  • 410 parts on a 6-layer, 80 × 60 mm board, with 0.1 % sense resistors for calibrated accuracy.

Engineering client, DACH2026FW

RS485 to LTE-M Gateway

Firmware for an RS485-to-cellular gateway in a remote measurement network. It relays SMS in both directions and uploads AES-encrypted measurement telegrams to a server.

  • RS485 slave on a proprietary 4-wire field-bus protocol. Frames are cut by their LEN field, so 10 ms inter-byte gaps and back-to-back frames from other stations are handled.
  • u-blox SARA-R4/R5 modem: LTE-M with NB-IoT and 2G fallback, and an automatic cold restart when the modem stops searching.
  • AES-128-CBC server upload, SMS relay and flash-backed configuration; STOP2 sleep with RS485 wake-up and an IWDG kept fed by a 20 s RTC wake-up.
  • Also the gateway bootloader and the PowerShell tool that flashes and configures stations from a PC.
  • Ported the measurement-station firmware to the SARA-R5: modem auto-detection, two-layer IP activation and a DMA receive fix that was dropping OK replies.

Agritech startup2025HWFW

FieldNode LoRaWAN Sensor

Battery-powered field sensor node for agriculture. It reads soil probes over RS485/Modbus and reports over LoRaWAN, idling at 2 µA. Six hardware revisions (v1.5.0 to v1.5.5) and firmware releases 51 to 91.

  • STM32L452 with a RAK3172 LoRaWAN module; TPS63900 low-Iq buck-boost, and a gated TPS61096 boost so the probe supply is off between readings.
  • FreeRTOS with tickless idle on LPTIM. Sleep is held off while a Modbus transaction is in flight.
  • Modbus RTU drivers for the JXBS-3001-RS soil probe (moisture, pH and more) and the TZ-THT02 sensor, with uplink formats per sensor.
  • JSON device configuration in LittleFS on external W25Q flash, a USB composite device for console and files, and a production test mode.
  • Designed around an IP-rated enclosure with cable glands, a pressure vent and a sub-GHz antenna.

Special vehicles OEM2023HWFW

Mining Rig Tool-Drive PSU

High-power DC/DC unit for the tool drive of an electric mining rig: 200–400 V in, two independent 24 V outputs at 42 A each. Rev 1.01 went into a production batch.

  • STM32F103 with CAN through a MAX33054, ACS37002 Hall-effect current sensing, an NTC input and E-stop voltage detection.
  • Per-channel wake-up relays and a current-share line that balances load between the two DC/DC stages.
  • Harting connector for HV and signals, M5 bushings for the 24 V outputs, TSR 1-2433 logic supply and a PSK-10D-24 auxiliary supply.
  • FreeRTOS firmware with separate main and CAN tasks, moving-average filtering and EEPROM emulation, plus a CAN bootloader for field updates.

Electrochemical Analysis of Carbon-Based Supercapacitors Using Finite Element Modeling and Impedance Spectroscopy

A. Azizpour, N. Bagovic, N. Ploumis, K. Mylonas, D. Hoxha, F. Kienberger, N. Al-Zubaidi-R-Smith, G. Gramse

Calibrated impedance spectroscopy and finite element modelling of carbon-based supercapacitors with ionic-liquid electrolytes. Cells with activated-carbon electrodes showed lower impedance than graphene ones, and the model matched the measurements from 10 Hz to 10 kHz.
